### About Us

Welcome to Ecua Serviced Accommodation, perfect for a relaxing getaway! An elegant home equipped with all the amenities you need for a comfortable stay. 

Situated in Dagenham in the Greater London region, this beautiful and Modern Semi-Detached 4 Bedroom House is 7 minutes from Dagenham Heathway Station, features accommodation with free WiFi and free private parking. 

The nearest airport is London City Airport, located 15 km from Stratford International. Guests can relax in the garden, dinning area indoors and outdoors, a spacious living room with a flat-screen TV, an equipped kitchen with oven and a microwave, The property has 3 bathrooms. A Double bedroom with a flat-screen TV and its own en-suite and kitchenette. Towels, bed linen and toiletries are available. For added privacy, the accommodation features a private entrance. This property is non-smoking. Available for short or long stays.

Dagenham Heathway train station is just a 7-minute walk away. This convenient transportation hub allows you to easily access nearby attractions and explore the bustling city of London. You'll find local convenience stores within walking distance for any last-minute essentials.
