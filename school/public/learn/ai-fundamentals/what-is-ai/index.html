What is AI? The short version

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines. It covers tasks that used to need a person: reasoning, understanding language, recognising patterns, learning from experience and making decisions.

AI isn't one program or one robot. It's an umbrella term for a whole field of computer science, with many techniques under it, from simple rule-based systems to machine learning and the large language models behind tools like ChatGPT and Claude.

Most AI systems work in a loop: they perceive the world through data (a self-driving car's cameras spot a person), reason about it (that's a pedestrian), act (slow down), and learn from the result so the next prediction is better.

Not every program is AI. A calculator follows fixed rules and never improves. A spam filter is AI: it learns from the emails it sees and gets more accurate with more data. The test is simple: does it adapt and make judgements, or only follow a fixed routine?
