##### Psalm 4

"I will lie down and sleep in peace, for you alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety."\
"When you are in your bed, search your heart and be silent."\
"Let the light of your face shine upon us, O LORD."

---

##### Psalm 6

"O LORD, heal me, for my bones are in agony. My soul is in anguish. How long, O LORD, how long?"

---

##### Psalm 7

"O LORD my God, I take refuge in you."  
"My shield is God Most High, who saves the upright in heart."

---

##### Psalm 8

"O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You have set your glory above the heavens."

---

##### Psalm 9

"I will praise you, O LORD, with all my heart. I will tell of all your wonders. I will sing praise to your name, O Most High."

"Those who know your name will trust you"

---

##### Psalm 11

"For the LORD is righteous, he loves justice; upright men will see his face."

---

##### Psalm 13

"How long will my enemy triumph over me?  
"Give light to my eyes."

"But I trust in your unfailing love, my heart rejoices in your salvation"

---


##### Psalm 16

"You have made known to me the path of life."

---

##### Psalm 18

"It’s God who arms me with strength and makes my way perfect."  
"I love you, O LORD, my strength"

"My God is my rock in whom I take refuge."
